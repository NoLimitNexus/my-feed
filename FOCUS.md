# My Feed: what he asked for

The only list of what is being built. Add every ask the turn he makes it; tick it when it ships.

## Why it exists (2026-10-14)
He is tired of algorithmic social feeds. He wants his own feed of only what he picks, to see and learn
new things, in control of it, with no mindless scrolling. "I want like an honest feed."

## Asks, 2026-10-14
- [x] His own feed page of the things he follows on X (GitHub projects, Three.js), newest first, on phone and PC.
- [x] Videos play right in the feed, like on X.
- [x] Pick categories (funny, learning, new things...) and control how much of each, including how much
      random stuff ("a small amount of random stuff ... I'd like to have full control").
- [x] Help finding sources: "I'm gonna need a lot of help finding things ... get exposed to new content".
- [x] Tune it: "I wanna see more of this, I want to see less of this".
- [x] Modes: sometimes more videos, sometimes more pictures ("sometimes I'm in a video mode").
- [x] Bookmark (save) things he likes.
- [x] Share things he likes. Long press is fine; keep the card interface as it is ("nice and clean").
- [x] Always new content to see; if that means dipping into other topics because his picks ran out,
      say so plainly on screen.
- [ ] Our own app he can install ("we can download this and have our own interface").
- [ ] Keep the current clean interface.
